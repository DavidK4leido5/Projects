# SwiftCart

Multi-store marketplace. Independent sellers open a store and list products; shoppers browse, keep a
cart, place an order. Sellers see their own catalogue. Admins see everything.

Non-goals: payment capture, shipping, tax. An order records what was reserved; no money moves.

## Surfaces

- Landing (marketplace grid): persuade, browse, find something worth opening.
- Product detail: decide.
- Cart and order history: operate.
- Seller dashboard and admin: operate.

## Audience

Shoppers who do not know any one store. They scan, filter, and compare. The page has to carry many
items at once without becoming a spreadsheet.
